# Platform Engineering Best Practices

Building a platform that developers love to use requires careful thought and attention to detail. Here are the key practices I've learned.

## Developer Experience First

The best platforms prioritize developer experience. If developers struggle to use your platform, they'll find workarounds or alternatives.

## Automation is Key

Automate everything that can be automated. Manual processes are error-prone and don't scale.

## Observability Matters

You can't improve what you can't measure. Comprehensive logging, monitoring, and alerting are essential.

## Security by Default

Security shouldn't be an afterthought. Build it into the platform from the beginning.

## Continuous Improvement

Platforms evolve. Regular reviews and improvements ensure they stay relevant and useful.

## Conclusion

Platform engineering is about enabling developers while maintaining operational excellence. Focus on developer experience, automate relentlessly, and always be improving.
